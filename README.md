# oer-everything

> Open K-12 curriculum library spanning math/science/reading/CS, mapped to curriculum standards.  ·  **Risk tier:** low  ·  **Status:** planning

A teacher in an under-resourced classroom does not lack open educational resources (OER) in the abstract — there are millions of them. They lack a way to *find the right one*: aligned to the exact standard they must teach, at the right grade band, genuinely free to use and adapt, accurate, and accessible. The open-education landscape is fragmented across hundreds of repositories with incompatible metadata, unverified or mislabeled licenses, dead links, and no consistent mapping to the curriculum standards teachers are actually accountable to. The result is a paradox of plenty: abundant open content, persistently low usable findability.

**Definition of shipped:** Curriculum library adopted by schools; standards-mapped.
